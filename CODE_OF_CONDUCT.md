# Community Code of Conduct

We want a welcoming space for contributors, including people making their first
contribution. This policy applies in project issues, pull requests,
discussions, and other project-managed spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient. Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Give specific, constructive feedback and accept corrections gracefully.
- Make room for different experience levels, backgrounds, and communication
  styles, including people who are new to open source and to Hacktoberfest.
- Respect privacy and obtain permission before sharing another person's
  information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, sexualized attention, personal
attacks, trolling, intimidation, and publishing private information without
consent are not acceptable. Repeated disruption after a request to stop is also
unacceptable.

## Reporting

Send a private report to **anasishaq7110@gmail.com**. If the report concerns a
maintainer, and you cannot reach an independent person through that address,
ask in an issue for an alternative private channel without naming the people
involved.

Include what happened, where and when it happened, relevant links or
screenshots, and your preferred way to be contacted. Share only information
needed to investigate. Avoid posting reports about individuals in public
issues. If anyone faces immediate danger, contact appropriate local emergency
services.

## Maintainer responsibilities and enforcement

Maintainers will review reports as privately as practical, listen to affected
people, and avoid retaliation. They may ask for more context and limit
information sharing to people who need it to respond. They cannot promise
absolute confidentiality.

Depending on the situation, maintainers may give a private warning, require a
change in behavior, remove content, temporarily restrict participation, or
permanently ban someone from project spaces. They may also decline to act when
a report is unsupported or outside project scope. Reporters and subjects should
be informed of outcomes when practical, without exposing another person's
private details.

The [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) is an optional
recognized standard maintainers may choose to adopt separately. This policy
does not claim to be a particular version of it.
